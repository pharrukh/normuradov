This is my no-[bullshit](https://archive.org/details/importanceofwhat00harr/page/117/mode/2up) corner, where you can see ideas I can produce and what thoughts occupy my mind. You may use it when you construct my ["Gamburg's Score"](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D0%93%D0%B0%D0%BC%D0%B1%D1%83%D1%80%D0%B3%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D1%81%D1%87%D1%91%D1%82) in a world full of titles, certifications and degrees.

My name is Farrukh. I'm a father, a husband and a son, born in Samarkand. I studied software engineering, and since March 2025 I have been a software engineer at Google, working on Google Cloud. I like the moment an idea becomes running code that saves someone time or makes their work easier.

This blog is about the role of an engineer, beyond the code itself. Posts here are long and infrequent; shorter notes go to my [Telegram channel](https://t.me/fnormuradov).

*Opinions here are my own and don't represent Google.*
